# The Field
## The Conceit
Water flowing off the mountain becomes streams or rivers in the Field. So, ideas are constrained, but flowing and aligned towards the eventual release of the project. First though, the flat and rolling expanse of the field allows for views of the project from near and far. Further, the gentle environment allows for a safe place to start bringing in gentle collaboration and feedback to nibble on the tender grass of the project in the field.
## The Objective
Early feedback, soft refinement, and legibility testing.
## How to Engage
Open the project to safe, trusted collaborators. Allow the work to be viewed from different distances to test its structural legibility and intuitive resonance.
## Beware This
Being so tied to the work that you can't disengage from what it is to help it become. The Field requires vulnerability; if you build a fence around the grass, intuitive discoveries cannot refine or impact the project.
## When to Move On
- [ ] After review you have found new mountains to climb (return to The Mountain)
- [ ] After review you are ready to move towards the ocean (go to The Delta)
