# The Mountain
## The Conceit
The burst of effort that is itself a small cycle: effort to summit and the equal or even harder effort to descend from those heights (emotionally and physically). Water here is available some at early and late stages of the climb but is locked in snow and ice (no new ideas) the closer you get to the summit. Repeat the climb and descent as many times as needed to be able to build.
## The Objective
A tighter cyclical sprint focused on summit then review.
## How to Engage
Building with absolute constraint. No new ideas. External inputs are frozen. Bring only what you will need. Focus entirely on the climb, making the summit (achieving a specific milestone) and then the deliberate physical/emotional descent and review.
## Beware This
On the way up: Trying to bring the whole forest with you, pulling new ideas and resources, causing avalanches and increasing weight (resistance).

On the way down: ignoring rest, recuperation, and review to move directly to a new summit, resulting in getting stuck through too much resistance and burnout.
## When to Move On
- [ ] The core milestone (the summit) has been achieved.
- [ ] The emotional and physical descent/recovery is acknowledged and managed.
- [ ] The raw product is ready to thaw and flow into the Field for outside eyes.
