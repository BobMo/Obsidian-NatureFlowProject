# The Fen
## The Conceit
This is very specifically a fen and not a swamp. Water enters a fen from hidden aquifers and springs to intermingle with a whole lot of life (which in this model represents external and internal resources available to the individual) in all aspects of life and death. 
## The Objective
Build wild prototypes with all of the fecund world about you.
## How to Engage
Unconstrained exploration. Ready access to ideas and resources, collecting from all manner of stimuli and places. Regularly dredge the contents of thhe `Fen_Resource` folder. Old composted projects and raw stimuli stored there act as the hidden aquifers feeding your new prototypes.
## Beware This
Staying in the Fen forever. It is comfortable to gather resources indefinitely, but without eventually constraining the flow, the project will stagnate and never take shape.
## When to Move On
- [ ] Core concept or prototype is established.
- [ ] Necessary internal and external resources are identified and gathered.
- [ ] The project has enough shape to withstand structural constraints.
